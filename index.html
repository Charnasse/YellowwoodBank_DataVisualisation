DATA CONTRACT & DEFINITIONS

Read the numbers with confidence

Source and reporting horizon

Deterministic synthetic customers, applications, claims, SARs and month-end holdings. Available activity: 1 Oct 2025 - 30 Sep 2026, the last 12 complete months at the October 2026 reference date. Earlier balance snapshots exist only for QoQ baselines. No live connection is configured.

Filter semantics

Branch means the customer's home branch; account type means the customer's primary account segment, including for loans, claims and SARs. Time filters select event dates for activity and month-end snapshots for holdings. Local filters never alter unrelated sections. Reset restores the full reporting year.

Stock versus flow

Approved loan amounts are flows recorded on approval dates, not outstanding debt. Account and relationship balances are stocks at the latest available month-end on or before the selected end date, within the window. A window without a month-end snapshot displays no balance data, not a fabricated zero.

Rates and comparisons

No denominator means N/A, not 0%. Loan and claim decisions after the selected end date remain pending at that cutoff. SAR resolution includes only resolutions on or before the end date; 24h counts use the same denominator of raised reports. QoQ compares each selected endpoint against the preceding full quarter-end for the same cohort; partial-quarter comparisons are labeled. Q4 2025 uses Sep 2025 as its baseline.

Customer value is a footprint, not profit

The combined figure sums unlike relationship measures as requested. Annual premiums are a yearly flow; deposits, loan principal and portfolio assets are stocks. It is not revenue, bank-owned assets, net worth or risk-adjusted profitability. Insurance coverage is excluded. No FX conversion or customer-level profitability model is implied.

Reconciliation and exports

Tables contain exact values supporting every chart. Exports include all filtered rows, not just the visible customer page. Loan exports contain both monthly and branch aggregates; insurance exports include policy and officer aggregates. Pending decisions are always visible.
