Management Insights — D(d)
INSIGHT 01
Q4 holds 30.7% of total deposits despite equal account distribution
The data warehouse roll-up query (OLAP) across DimDate reveals that Q4 2026 holds R 622,193 — the highest quarterly balance at 30.7% of total deposits. Q2 is significantly the weakest quarter at R 392,246 (19.4%). This is not driven by the number of accounts but by the higher-value accounts opened in Q4, particularly the Private and Business type accounts linked to DimBranch (Nelspruit and Polokwane), which average R 97,000+ per account.
MANAGEMENT DECISION
Launch targeted Q2 deposit campaigns — promotional savings rates or business account incentives — to close the gap between Q2 and Q4 performance. Management could also investigate whether the Q4 spike is driven by year-end bonuses, which would be cyclical and should inform product timing strategy for 2027.
INSIGHT 02
20% of loan repayments are overdue — credit risk requires immediate attention
Out of 80 loan repayment records loaded into FactTransaction from LOAN_REPAYMENT, 20 (25%) are marked Overdue and a further 20 (25%) are Pending. Only 40 repayments (50%) are fully Paid. The overdue repayments span loans 2001–2020, with overdue payments concentrated on Card repayment method entries. This signals a systemic risk in the Card payment channel and represents potential non-performing loan exposure across the DimLoan dimension.
MANAGEMENT DECISION
Implement automated debit order switching for Card-method repayments where arrears exceed 30 days. The credit analyst department should be notified of all Overdue statuses flagged in DimLoan for proactive customer engagement. Management should also set an NPL (non-performing loan) KPI threshold in the dashboard — e.g. overdue rate must remain below 15% — and trigger an escalation workflow when it is breached.
